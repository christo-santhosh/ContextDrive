\chapter{Conclusion}

The Farming Intelligence AI project provides an integrated smart farming platform that combines IoT-based soil monitoring, machine learning, agricultural advisory, Explainable AI, market intelligence, and a retrieval-augmented generation assistant. By using soil and environmental data, the system helps farmers identify suitable crops and receive fertilizer, irrigation, and agricultural guidance. The integration of explainable AI improves the transparency of crop recommendations, while market intelligence supports better economic decision-making. The RAG-based assistant further provides accessible agricultural information through a knowledge-based approach. Overall, the project aims to support farmers in making informed, data-driven decisions and adopting more efficient and sustainable farming practices.
